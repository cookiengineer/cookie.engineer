Blue Team

This learning path focuses on defending systems, investigating attacks, and building reliable detections.

Rather than concentrating on individual security products, the articles explain the underlying concepts so they remain applicable regardless of the tools used.

It is highly recommended to complete the Fundamentals learning path before beginning this one.
