Cyber Security Learning Path

These are the major learning paths, it is recommended to read them step by step, as they're structured in a way that knowledge for later topics is documented earlier in the course.

  1. Fundamentals covers the technological fundamentals.
  2. Strategies covers the mindset of cyber warfare.
  3. Redteam covers the Red Team operator's learning path.
  4. Blueteam covers the Blue Team defender's learning path.
  5. Sysinternals covers advanced Operating System internals.
  6. Reverse Engineering covers advanced Reverse Engineering topics.
  7. Privilege Escalation covers advanced Privilege Escalation exploits.
