Strategies

This learning path teaches the strategic dimension of cyber warfare.

Learning Path

This learning path examines cyber security not as a collection of tools and techniques, but as a domain of strategic thought. It draws upon millennia of military philosophy, from Sun Tzu to Clausewitz and Boyd, and reinterprets their principles for an era where conflict is waged through network packets, malware implants, memory corruption, and C2 operations.

The goal is not to teach exploitation or defense. Those skills are covered in the Red Team and Blue Team learning paths. Instead, this course asks the questions that sit above technique:

  • Why do certain strategies succeed?
  • When is an attack justified?
  • What does victory mean in a domain without attribution?

It is recommended to complete the Fundamentals learning path before beginning this one. The philosophical arguments assume familiarity with networking, operating systems, exploit primitives, and the architecture of modern botnet infrastructure.
