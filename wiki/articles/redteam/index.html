Red Team

This learning path focuses on offensive security from the perspective of understanding systems and exploiting weaknesses.

The goal is not to memorize tools, but to understand why each technique works and how it can be reproduced manually.

It is highly recommended to complete the Fundamentals learning path before beginning this one.
