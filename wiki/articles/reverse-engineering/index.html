Reverse Engineering

This learning path focuses on analyzing unknown binaries on Linux — from static triage through dynamic instrumentation to full decompilation.

The goal is not to memorize tool GUIs, but to understand what happens at the instruction level and how to reconstruct high-level intent from low-level artifacts. Every article explains the underlying mechanism before introducing the tool that automates it.

It is highly recommended to complete the Techniques learning path before beginning this one. The reverse engineering articles assume familiarity with ELF structure, process memory layout, the stack, calling conventions, and system calls.

Before executing anything, extract every byte of information the binary voluntarily provides.
