Cookie's Cyber Wiki

Hello, fr1end.

This is Cookie's Cyber Wiki, where I try to manifest my knowledge about cyber security for good. In my previous decades I made the mistake of posting a lot on social media or in web forums. Yeah, I know, all that knowledge is gone and indiscoverable because of the generative AI disease.

Currently, I'm still working on a couple of other projects. After that I want to spend more time on migrating, proofreading my backported articles, and fixing the missing Cyber Wiki articles.

I also organize a weekly learning group that is based on this Cyber Wiki's contents and we love to do CTF s together where we implement the exploits ourselves from scratch.

If you want to join us in the learning group or if you've found bugs in this Cyber Wiki's contents, please let me know and Contact Me .

So long, and thanks for all the fish!
